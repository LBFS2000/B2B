FreshBooks vs Xero: Multi-Currency & Bank Reconciliation

Executive Verdict

Choose FreshBooks if you are a service-based freelancer requiring simple, intuitive invoicing and time-tracking. Choose Xero if you operate a scaling business that demands robust multi-currency accounting and advanced automated bank reconciliation.

⚠️ Hidden Gotchas & Integration Limits

FreshBooks hides multi-currency capabilities behind custom enterprise-level Select plans, making it unsuitable for international trade without significant negotiation. Xero's Early plan limits users to 20 invoices and 5 bills per month, which can trigger unexpected upgrade costs as transaction volume grows. Both platforms utilize third-party payroll integrations (Gusto for Xero, FreshBooks Payroll) that incur additional per-user monthly fees not reflected in the base subscription price.
