⚠️ Hidden Gotchas & Integration Limits

Architects must evaluate the stark contrast between daily and minute-based rate limits. monday.com enforces a strict daily ceiling of 25000 API calls even on its highest standard enterprise tier, which can break high-volume webhook integrations. ClickUp offers 10000 calls per minute on Enterprise, making it vastly superior for real-time data synchronization. However, ClickUp forces workspace-wide upgrades for all members, meaning adding even one admin or contributor immediately triggers billing recalculations for the entire seat count.
