⚠️ Hidden Gotchas & Integration Limits

Rippling's pricing is highly variable based on the specific modules enabled, making total cost of ownership difficult to forecast without a signed contract. Deel's promotional offers, such as the 3-month free PEO credit, often contain clawback clauses requiring full repayment of list price fees if the contract is terminated early. Both platforms utilize proprietary API layers that may limit deep integration with legacy ERP systems without custom middleware.
