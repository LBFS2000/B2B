⚠ Contract Lock-In, Add-On Fees & API Rate Gotchas

Rippling's pricing is highly variable based on the number of modules enabled, often leading to unexpected costs when scaling IT management. Deel's 'no hidden fees' marketing masks the fact that promotional credits for PEO services are tied to strict two-year contract lock-ins; terminating early triggers immediate repayment of all waived fees. Both platforms utilize rate-limited APIs that can throttle high-frequency syncs with custom internal ERPs during peak payroll cycles.
