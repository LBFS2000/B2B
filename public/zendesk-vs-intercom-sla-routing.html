Is Zendesk or Intercom cheaper for a 25-user team in 2026? +

At 25 active seats, Zendesk costs approximately $1375/month ($0/mo base + $55/seat), whereas Intercom costs approximately $975/month ($0/mo base + $39/seat). Over a 12-month period, Intercom saves approximately $4,800 USD (or A$7,392 AUD) before annual billing discounts.
