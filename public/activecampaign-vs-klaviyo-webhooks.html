⚠ Contract Lock-In, Add-On Fees & API Rate Gotchas

When migrating between ActiveCampaign and Klaviyo, procurement teams frequently overlook historical audit-trail export limits, SAML SSO 'SSO-tax' tier upgrades, and multi-currency FX settlement spreads (typically 0.5% to 2.0% above mid-market rates). Always verify whether sandbox API environments and dedicated webhook retry queues are included in your base commercial tier before signing an annual order form.
