Gusto vs OnPay: Xero Sync & Tax Filing Comparison

Executive Verdict

Choose Gusto if you require a robust, feature-rich ecosystem with deep Xero integration and advanced HR automation. Select OnPay if you prioritize a lower-cost, flat-fee structure with comprehensive tax filing included for all users.

Gusto Breakdown

  • ✅ Superior Xero integration with detailed general ledger mapping
  • ✅ Extensive public API for custom workflow automation
  • ✅ Advanced HR compliance tools and R&D tax credit scanning
  • ❌ Higher per-employee costs as headcount scales
  • ❌ Premium features like same-day pay require higher-tier plans

OnPay Breakdown

  • ✅ Predictable, transparent pricing model with no hidden add-on fees
  • ✅ White-glove data migration services included for new clients
  • ✅ Strong focus on accountant-partner revenue sharing
  • ❌ Less sophisticated accounting integration compared to Gusto
  • ❌ Limited self-service API capabilities for non-partners

⚠️ Hidden Gotchas & Integration Limits

Gusto utilizes a tiered pricing structure where advanced features like same-day pay or compliance tools are gated behind higher monthly subscriptions, potentially increasing costs significantly as you scale. OnPay offers a more stable price point, but users often find the integration depth with third-party accounting software like Xero less granular, requiring more manual reconciliation for complex ledger entries.
