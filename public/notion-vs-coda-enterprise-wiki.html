⚠ Contract Lock-In, Add-On Fees & API Rate Gotchas

Notion enforces a strict 3 requests per second limit on its public API, which creates significant bottlenecks for automated data synchronization in large environments. Coda offers higher throughput but often requires custom 'Packs' development to handle complex authentication, which can lead to hidden maintenance overhead. Both platforms lock advanced security features behind Enterprise tiers, and buyers should be wary of 'per-seat' pricing models that inflate rapidly when scaling guest access or external collaborator accounts.
