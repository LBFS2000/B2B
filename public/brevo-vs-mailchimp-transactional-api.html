⚠ Contract Lock-In, Add-On Fees & API Rate Gotchas

Mailchimp enforces strict contact-based billing, meaning you pay for inactive subscribers, which can lead to massive cost spikes as your list grows. Brevo's transactional SMTP is powerful but requires careful domain authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) setup to avoid deliverability issues, which is often underestimated by teams migrating from simpler platforms. Both platforms have hidden overage fees if you exceed monthly send limits, but Mailchimp's overages are significantly more aggressive regarding account suspension.
