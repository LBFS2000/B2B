Testing & Benchmarking Methodology

How We Audit Enterprise SaaS Platforms

Every head-to-head comparison published on our platform undergoes a structured 4-part technical audit:

  • REST API & Webhook Inspection: We review official developer documentation to verify request-per-minute throttling, OAuth2 scopes, and webhook retry backoff logic.
  • ERP & Accounting Ledger Fidelity: We verify whether integrations with Xero, QuickBooks Online, and NetSuite operate via native two-way sync or require third-party middleware.
  • Security & Governance Verification: We check public trust centers for active SOC2 Type II attestations, ISO 27001 certification, HIPAA BAA availability, and SAML SSO gating.
  • Deterministic TCO Modeling: We extract base platform fees and per-seat licensing tiers to power our interactive headcount cost calculators.
