About B2BComparison.com

Independent B2B Software & TCO Research Publication

B2BComparison.com is an independent software documentation review and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) modeling publication built for CTOs, Finance Directors, RevOps leaders, and SMB/Mid-Market procurement teams across Australia, APAC, and North America.

Why We Built B2BComparison.com

Most software directories rely on subjective star-ratings and omit the commercial and technical details that drive total cost: REST API rate ceilings, webhook retry queues, SAML SSO tier gating, and regional tax/payroll compliance (such as Australian ATO Single Touch Payroll Phase 2, Payday Super 2026, Fair Work Award interpretation, and two-way Xero/MYOB/QuickBooks ledger reconciliation).
