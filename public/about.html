About B2BComparison Research Lab

Independent B2B Software Architecture & TCO Audits

B2BComparison.com is an independent software benchmarking and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) modeling platform built for CTOs, Finance Directors, RevOps leaders, and SMB/Mid-Market procurement teams across Australia, APAC, and North America.

Why We Built B2BComparison.com

Most software review directories rely on subjective user star-ratings and hide the technical realities that cause implementation failures: REST API rate ceilings, webhook retry bottlenecks, SAML SSO tier gating, and regional tax/payroll compliance (such as Australian ATO Single Touch Payroll Phase 2, Payday Super, and two-way Xero/QuickBooks general ledger mapping).
